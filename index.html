FAQ

Questions, answered

What happens if the battery runs out?

You'll get a low-battery warning days before. If it does run empty, you can power the lock from any USB-C power bank to open it.

How many fingerprints can I register?

Up to 10 fingerprints, so you can add several fingers or trusted people.

Is it allowed as cabin baggage on flights?

Yes. All models fit standard cabin size limits, and the lock battery is within airline rules for carry-on items.

Do you ship across Europe?

Yes, we ship to all EU countries, plus the UK, Switzerland and Norway.

Can I return it?

Yes. You have 14 days after delivery to return your bag, no reason needed.
