Layers of protection

Designed against the way bags get stolen

Pickpockets open zips, thieves slash fabric, and lost bags are hard to find. Each model combines several of these defences.

Fingerprint lock

The zip pullers lock into a biometric sensor. Only a registered fingerprint opens it: no code to forget, no key to lose.

Cut-resistant fabric

A slash-resistant layer stops "cut and grab" thefts from behind, in crowded trains or at a café table.

Tracking built in

Selected models work with Apple Find My or carry a positioning module, so you can locate the bag if it goes missing.

RFID-blocking pocket

A shielded pocket protects contactless bank cards from unauthorised scanning.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Which bags have a fingerprint lock?

The MetroSecure Pro backpack, the Shield Crossbody, the Bio Clutch, the Bio Crossbody and the Heritage Crossbody. Use the "Fingerprint lock" filter in the shop to see them.

Are the bags waterproof?

They are water-resistant: the fabric repels rain and splashes, so your laptop stays dry on the way to work. They are not designed to be submerged in water.

Can I track my bag if it is lost or stolen?

The MetroSecure Pro has a dedicated pocket for Apple Find My tracking, and the SecureTrack Pack has a built-in positioning feature.

Will my laptop fit?

Every product page lists the dimensions. The Stealth Pack and our business backpacks fit 15.6" laptops, and the business handbag fits a 16" MacBook Pro.

Do you ship across Europe?

Yes, we ship to all EU countries.

Can I return my bag?

Yes. You have 14 days after delivery to return it, no reason needed.
